## User flow (verify in `public/js/app.js`)
1. Pick project, one housing type (`Scoring.HOUSING_TYPES`), blocks, storey range, floor priority (higher / lower / none; **default Higher**, `loadProject` and the `checked` radio in `build_pages.py`, `restoreLast` restores filters and weights kept in this browser but NOT the floor priority: it always starts at Higher, because an old saved choice (e.g. Lower from an earlier visit) surprised a user. The scoring direction is correct: with `higher` the top storey of a block scores highest; e2e checks both), importance -5..+5 per factor.
2. Press **Rank units**. Only when `ADS_ENABLED` is true: the first time in a browser session (sessionStorage `mybto:ad-consent`) a pop-up opens with a placeholder ad slot (300x250, no third-party script) and a "Show my ranking" button (no research notice text any more; consent is still recorded as before) that turns on after a 5 second countdown ("Not now" cancels). Agreeing is the consent (`consent: true`, `consented_at`). There is no survey and no queue number. The ad logic lives in `public/js/ads.js` (`MyBTOAds.interstitial()` and `MyBTOAds.rewarded(projectKey)`); the single place to plug in a real ad network is `PROVIDERS` in that file. Tests may shorten countdowns with `window.MYBTO_AD_SECONDS = { interstitial, rewarded }`; production is 5 s and 15 s.
3. Ranking shows the **top 100** (`SHOW_TOP`); the **full order** of matching units is sent to the sheet (`ranking` = list of unit ids). The Sheet's `top50` column name and size are unchanged.
4. Button above the ranking: **Shortlisted** (`/shortlist`, opens in a new tab; star units, drag or arrows to reorder). The Analytics button is archived (see switches).
5. Shortlist page (`renderShortlist` in `app.js`): shortlist per project in `flags:<project>`, kept across visits and synced across tabs (`storage` event). After each Rank press `rememberRanking` stores `mybto:lastrank:<project>` = `{at, desc, ids, ranks, scores, prev}` (full ordered ranking; `prev` = the ranking before, only replaced when the result changes). Each shortlisted unit shows "Rank 12 (was 30) · score +4.5" from it (rank shown even beyond the top 100; "not in your latest ranking" if filtered out), plus a note with the parameters and time. **Clear all** asks `Are you sure you want to clear your whole shortlist for <Project>?` (Cancel keeps it) and syncs to the Sheet. **Share shortlist** builds `location.origin/shortlist?project=<key>&units=<ids,comma,in order>` (max 100), uses `navigator.share` if present, else copies and says "Link copied". Opening a link shows a read-only view with banner "Shared shortlist for <Project>", **Save as my shortlist** (confirm, replaces theirs, syncs) and **Back to my shortlist**; ids are checked against the project's units (unknown ignored, cap 100) and the visitor's own list is never changed unless they save.
- Ranks are dense: equal scores share a rank and the next score gets the next number (1, 1, 2). Ties break by higher storey then id. Rows with score below 0 are shaded below a red line.
- Facings are only NE, SE, SW, NW. Sun direction means where the living room windows face.
- Chute factor: unit is **more than 10 m** from the nearest refuse chute (`CHUTE_FAR_M = 10`, `chute_m > 10`).
- Score = sum of importance x feature (0..1). Factors in `public/js/scoring.js`: block, sun, design (2-Room Flexi Type 1/2), clearance (>30 m from nearest visible unit), corner, roof, chute (label "10 m away from rubbish chute"), mrt (label "Within 100 m from MRT"; `MRT_NEAR_M = 100` in both pipelines), mscp (label "Within 100 m from a multi-storey car park", `mscp_near`, `MSCP_NEAR_M = 100` in both pipelines and `Scoring`), wind_facing (label "Faces the prevailing wind (SE to E)": 1 for NE or SE facing, because both catch east wind; Singapore's wind is from SE to E, bearing 90 to 135), wind_open (label "Not blocked from the wind", unit field `wind_open`: no other block within 30 m on the SE to E side), facilities (has = 1, near = 0.5; one meter per facility, see Facilities below), floor (fixed weight 5). A meter shows only when some unit in scope has that feature. Importance panel order (`renderWeights` in `app.js`, groups in `Scoring.GROUPS`): Blocks, Sun direction, Wind direction (wind_facing, wind_open), Unit design (2-Room Flexi only), Distance (clearance, chute, mrt, mscp), Others (corner, roof), Facilities nearby (within 30 m). A group shows only if it has an available factor. The legend drawer (`legend.js`) is sorted into the same order.
- localStorage keys: `project`, `flags:<project>` (shortlist), `mybto:flagcount`, `mybto:uid` (anonymous random user id, starts with `u`), `mybto:last:<project>` (last filters and weights, restored on return; also drives the rank shown on the shortlist page). Always wrapped in try/catch.

## Real-data decisions (agreed with the user)
- Roof access: "roof at 2nd storey only" on HDB plans is a non-accessible roof slab, so it is NOT roof access (`roof_floors` = []). Stacks beside such a roof may carry `roof_next_to_2nd_storey_roof` / `roof_note_2nd_storey`. Car-park roof gardens reached by shared link bridges do not count either. Result: no unit in the 7 projects has roof access, so the "Access to roof" meter hides itself once real data is published.
- More than 30 m: a unit fails if any red is inside a board semicircle centred on that unit; a neighbour's red overlapping the unit's own green semicircle does not fail it.
- Block-wide (user rule 2026-10-13): facilities_near, MRT within 100 m (`mrt_m`) and car park (`mscp_m`) are per BLOCK. A facility's 30 m circle touching any part of the block outline gives it to every unit in the block (any touch counts). `facilities_has` wins over near. Per-unit only: gt30m, facing, position, chute_m (to the general chute, within 10 m means <= 10), wind_open (from the living-room window wall, 90 to 135 deg inclusive). Each `pipeline/raw/real/<key>.json` keeps a `blocks` list.
- Real data is live for all 7 projects (6,952 units). After editing `pipeline/raw/real/*.json`: `python3 pipeline/build_real_data.py` (review sheet in `review/`, gitignored), then `--publish`. The roof legend entry was removed (no unit has roof access).

## Recipes
**Change page copy.** Rank/shortlist/analytics/index text: edit `pipeline/build_pages.py`, run `python3 pipeline/build_pages.py`, commit both. Copy built in JS: `public/js/app.js` (`FACTOR_HELP` texts near the top), `site.js`, `legend.js`. Archive home: edit `public/archive/home.html`.

**Change a factor or scoring.** Edit `public/js/scoring.js` (`FACTORS`, `scoreUnit`, `availability`), the matching text and meters in `app.js`, a legend entry in `legend.js`, then update `test/scoring.test.js` expectations (if you are allowed to touch tests). If factor keys change, update `FACTOR_KEYS` in `apps-script/Analytics.js` (a test compares it to `Scoring.FACTORS`; the Rankings header gets a new `weight_<key>` column, so also fix the header in the live sheet and redeploy the Apps Script). Rerun `npm run sample` if sample weights depend on it. Run all tests.

**Regenerate data.** Sample: `python3 pipeline/make_sample_data.py && npm run sample`. Real: `python3 pipeline/build_dataset.py` (exits non-zero and prints `BUILD FAILED` on missing positions). Commit `public/data/units/` and `public/data/sample_submissions.json`. After a deploy, run `importSample()` again in Apps Script if the sample changed.

**Real data (per-unit analysis).** 1) Run the per-project analysis (brochure + annotated board) so it writes `pipeline/raw/real/<project_key>.json` (schema: stacks with facing, gt30m + evidence, position, chute_m, roof_floors, wind_open, mscp_m, mrt_m, facilities; units with block/storey/unit/flat_type; `totals_check`). 2) `python3 pipeline/build_real_data.py` validates (missing files are skipped with a warning) and writes `review/mybto-units-review.xlsx` (Summary, one sheet per project with one row per stack, All units) and `.csv`. 3) Give the review workbook to the user. 4) Only after the user approves, `python3 pipeline/build_real_data.py --publish`, run `npm test`, commit `public/data/units/`. Projects without a real file keep their sample file and stay in `index.json`. Real units get `lift_m`/`neighbour_m`/`opposite_m` null, `opposite_gt30m` = `gt30m`, `sample_fields` []. Do not hand-edit `pipeline/raw/real/*.json` while another agent is writing them.

**Facilities (void deck and future).** Real entries come from `pipeline/raw/facilities_1st_storey.json` (site plan legend markers plus the unit distribution pages, which label the 1st storey of a block). In `make_sample_data.py` a facility in a listed block goes to `facilities_has` of that block's units, and to `facilities_near` of the project's other blocks; entries with `block: null` (standalone MSCP or low-rise blocks such as 325 or 436, or legend-only items) go to `facilities_near` for every unit of the project. They are applied after the random draw, so other attributes do not reshuffle. Random placeholders use `FACILITIES_RANDOM`; "Mini-mart" and "Future amenities/facilities" are never random. "Future amenities/facilities" is explained in `FUTURE_FAC_HELP` (`app.js`, meter tooltip) and in the facilities entry of `legend.js`. `build_dataset.py` merges the same JSON.

**Wind and car park data.** `wind_open` in `make_sample_data.py` is a placeholder (own seeded Random `bto-sample-wind-<key>`, NE/SE stacks 85% open, others 50%; in `SAMPLE_FIELDS`). In `build_dataset.py` it is an explicit raw stack field `wind_open` if present, else computed: no stack of another block within `WIND_OPEN_M = 30` at bearing 90 to 135 (uses `site["north_bearing_deg"]`, default 0 = plan up is north; TODO footprints instead of stack points). `mscp_m` comes from `mscp_distance.json` per block; to refine, edit that file and regenerate. Legend pictures `lg-wind-facing`, `lg-wind-open`, `lg-mscp` are PIL drawings over brochure crops (12 legend entries; the e2e test counts them).

**Add a facility.** Brochure facility: add an entry to `pipeline/raw/facilities_1st_storey.json` (block null if you cannot tell the block), regenerate. Sample placeholder: add the name to `FACILITIES_RANDOM` in `pipeline/make_sample_data.py`. Real data: add it to the site-plan extraction in `pipeline/raw/` (`build_dataset.py` reads `site["facilities"]`, near = within 50 m). Regenerate data. Facility meters appear automatically; add a legend note if needed.

**Add a project.** Add to `PROJECTS` in `pipeline/make_sample_data.py` and `build_dataset.py`, regenerate, check `public/data/units/index.json`, update README project table and this skill.
