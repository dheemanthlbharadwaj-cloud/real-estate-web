One last step to see your ranking

This tool is part of a final year research project at the National University of Singapore (NUS), built using data from the June 2026 BTO launch. Before showing your ranking, we’d like to ask 10 short questions (1-2 minutes).

How old are you?
Who will you live with in the new flat? (Select all that apply)
What is your application status?
Where do you live now?
How many times have you applied for a BTO before?
Where do you mainly work or study?
Do you own or plan to own a car?
Do you plan to have children in the next 5 years?
What is your monthly household income?
How would you rate your queue position (if you have one)?

By selecting “I agree, show my ranking”, you confirm that you are 21 or older and agree to your answers and ranking being stored anonymously and used only for this NUS research project. Questions?
