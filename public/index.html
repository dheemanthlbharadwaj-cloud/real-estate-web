How it works

From brochure to shortlist in three steps.

No spreadsheets, no squinting at floor plans. Tell us what you care about and we do the arithmetic for every unit.

01

Set your filters

Choose a project, the flat types and blocks you're considering, your floor range — and switch on the privacy filter if you'd rather not look into a neighbour's window.

02

Weigh what matters

Slide each factor from −5 to +5: north–south facing, corner unit, distance from the lift or rubbish chute, the MRT, facilities next door.

03

Shortlist & save

Every unit is scored and ranked. Star your favourites into a shortlist you can reorder, and save it with your queue number.

What we measure

The details that make a unit yours.

Each measurement is taken from the official drawings, using each plan's own scale bar.

Sun direction

Which way the living room faces — eight compass points.

Floor level

Higher, middle or lower — relative to each block's height.

Privacy

Whether the unit you face is more than 30 m away.

Corner unit

End-of-row homes with windows on two sides.

Lift distance

Metres from your door to the lift lobby.

Rubbish chute

How far the refuse chute is from your front door.

Near MRT

Blocks within a five-minute walk of a station.

Facilities

Playgrounds, fitness corners, shops — in or beside your block.
