03How it works

From brochure to shortlist in three steps.

No spreadsheets and no squinting at floor plans. Tell us what you care about and we do the maths for every unit.

i.

Filter

Choose a project, the flat types and blocks you would consider and your floor range. Turn on the privacy filter if you would rather not look into a neighbour's window.

ii.

Weigh

Slide each factor from minus 5 to plus 5: facing, corner unit, distance from the lift or chute, the MRT and facilities next door.

iii.

Shortlist

Every unit gets a score and a rank. Star your favourites, put them in order and save the list with your queue number.
