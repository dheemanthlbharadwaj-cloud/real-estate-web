Corridor or corner unit

One meter per facility
Facilities nearby
“Has” means the facility is in your own block, for example at its first storey or on its roof. “Near” means it is not inside any residential block but sits within 50 m of your unit. Each facility type found in your selected blocks gets its own meter, so you can want a supermarket close by and keep a hardcourt away. A “has” counts fully and a “near” counts half.
Source: numbered markers on the site plan and its legend.
